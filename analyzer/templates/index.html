▾ How the score works

Each measured recipe gets one score (lower is better): distance from the target size plus a spread penalty. The next-recipe method uses it to pick the next recipe.

  • Score (size error plus spread); one tolerance off the target counts as 1. score = ((R−Rt)/tol)² + w·(PDI/cap); you enter a diameter, the optimiser works in radius: Rt = diameter/2, tol = diameter tolerance/2.
  • PDI is σ/R̄ of the fitted Schulz distribution, not the DLS polydispersity index.
  • Held results (not the expected shape) count against the budget but do not steer the search until you accept them in Fitting.
